    Mapping value alignment

    A distant reading of how eight AI companies articulate desirable conduct, potential harms, training interventions, and evaluations in model-card documentation.

    The AI alignment stack

    Alignment moves downward through a documentary and technical stack: public rules frame corporate commitments; those commitments define desirable conduct and risks; training operationalises them; and benchmarking makes them measurable.

    Introduction

    Value alignment is not a single technical intervention. It is a chain of choices through which companies define desirable conduct and potential harms, train models to respond to them, and evaluate whether those responses hold. This map follows that chain across model cards and related alignment documents.

    Rather than treating safety policies, system cards, training methods and evaluations as separate artefacts, we trace their connections. A stated value or risk becomes operational when it is translated into a training intervention and made legible through a test. The map therefore treats documentation as evidence of what companies disclose about this chain—not as a complete account of how a model is actually trained or governed.

    Method

    The corpus contains model cards, system cards, safety frameworks, policy pages and technical reports from OpenAI, Google, Anthropic, xAI, Meta, DeepSeek, Mistral AI and Microsoft. Each source record links a value or risk to its definition, reported training method and reported benchmark where these are disclosed.

    Constancy is measured within each company and virtue/risk item. The score combines linguistic similarity among its definitions, recurrence across distinct documents, and persistence over the company’s documentary timeline. A recently introduced or rarely mentioned item is therefore treated as variable even when its few definitions happen to use similar language.

    From company to evaluation

    The rankflow follows the alignment stack from company, to thematic category, to training category, to benchmark category. Flow widths preserve source-record weights. Colours follow each flow’s source at a given stage, in the style of a RAWGraphs alluvial diagram. Hover a line to isolate it and highlight both endpoints.

    Undisclosed training and benchmarking records are omitted from this global view to keep the disclosed associations legible.

    Limitations

    The map captures published descriptions, which vary substantially in scope, detail and genre. A company with many documents will naturally produce more source records; the alluvial preserves this empirical distribution while the matrices make the documentary basis visible. Absence from a matrix means absence from the collected disclosure, not necessarily absence from the model-development process.
